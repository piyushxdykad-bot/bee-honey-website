Four hives.One stubbornidea.

In 2014 we kept four hives behind a coffee estate in Coorg. The idea was simple: honey should taste like the place it came from, and it should leave the bees better off than it found them. Twelve years later, nothing about that has changed.

What we believe

We believe honey is a place in a jar. The flowers, the season and the hands that kept the hive should all be in the taste. So we never heat it, never blend it, and never take more than the bees can spare. Slow honey, honestly made.

The journey

Twelve years, one hive at a time.

2014 / 2026

2014

Four hives in Coorg

Our first colonies sat between rows of coffee plants. That spring, coffee blossom honey was the first jar we ever filled.

4 hives · 1 family
2016

The first market stall

Forty jars on a folding table at the Madikeri Sunday market. They were gone by noon, and people came back asking what flower it was.

40 jars · sold out
2018

Keepers join us

Six family beekeepers in Karnataka and Himachal joined. We agreed on one rule: pay fairly, and pay before the harvest, not after.

7 apiaries · 2 states
2021

Every lid, traceable

We started lab testing every batch and printing an apiary code on each lid, so you can see exactly whose hives your honey came from.

100% of batches tested
2024

How we work

Four promises, kept.

These are the rules every apiary signs up to. They shape every jar, and we check them every season.

PROMISE 01

Bees come first.

We leave at least a third of every hive’s honey for winter, and never feed sugar syrup to boost a harvest.

Honey left in each hive≥ 33%
Colonies, not quotasHives are split naturally in spring, never pushed.
PROMISE 02

Raw, always.

Never heated above 35°C. Never blended across seasons.

PROMISE 03

Traceable to the hive.

Every lid carries an apiary code and harvest month. Every batch is tested for purity and moisture.

PROMISE 04

Fair to keepers.

Paid before harvest, at a price agreed together each year.
