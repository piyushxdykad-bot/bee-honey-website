    Help the bees near you

    Plant something for the bees.

    Bees need flowers close to home, and Indian cities are full of balconies. Pick your space to see what grows well and feeds bees.

    Skip the spraysPesticides harm bees most. Neem oil after sunset, if you must.
    Leave a little waterA shallow dish with pebbles so bees can land and drink.
    Let herbs flowerTulsi and coriander in bloom are bee favourites.
