    Good to know

    Questions people ask us.

    Still wondering about something? Write to us at hello@beehoney.in and a beekeeper will reply.

    My honey has turned thick and grainy. Has it gone bad?

    No. That is crystallisation, and it is a sign the honey is raw. Stand the jar in warm water (not hot, below 40°C) for 15 minutes and stir. It will turn smooth again.

    How should I store it?

    Keep it at room temperature, lid closed, away from direct sun. Don’t refrigerate it, and use a dry spoon so no water gets in.

    How long does it keep?

    Stored well, honey keeps for years. We print a best-before date two years from harvest, along with the apiary code and harvest month.

    Can I give honey to my baby?

    Please don’t give any honey, raw or processed, to children under 12 months old. Their digestion isn’t ready for it yet. From their first birthday onwards it’s fine.

    Can I add a gift note to more than one jar?

    Yes. Each gift in your basket keeps its own note, and we write every one out by hand before it ships.
